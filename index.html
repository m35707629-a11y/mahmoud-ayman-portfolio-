01 / About

Turning logs into decisions

I'm a Cybersecurity & SOC Analyst with a strong technical foundation in networking, firewalls, and security monitoring — built through focused academic and industry training rather than surface-level courses.

My work centers on translating raw security data into clear, actionable insight: from configuring FortiGate and FortiSIEM environments to building correlation rules that catch abnormal activity before it becomes a problem.

I'm especially drawn to helping startups and small businesses, who often can't justify a full in-house security team but still need a technically sound line of defense. Whether it's reviewing a network's security posture, training a team to spot phishing, or tracing an incident through the logs, I bring the same standard: precise, well-documented, and genuinely useful — not generic advice repackaged as a service.
